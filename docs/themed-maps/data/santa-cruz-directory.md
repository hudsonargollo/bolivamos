## Data notes

- Order follows TripAdvisor's popularity ranking at the time of scraping. Ratings are TripAdvisor bubble scores (out of 5); "—" means not yet rated or not shown.
- One exact pagination duplicate (Cafe Buena Vista) was removed, leaving 359 unique restaurant listings out of the 360 results TripAdvisor reports. Entries marked "(2nd location)" / "(2nd listing)" appear as separate listings on TripAdvisor — most are real branches, a few may be duplicate registrations worth verifying.
- The three Attraction_Products URLs (zfg11866, zfg11881, zfg11876) are blocked from automated access by TripAdvisor's robots rules; their tour products are the same items already captured in Section 2 from the Tours category pages.
- One misplaced TripAdvisor listing ("Jeddah Airport to Mecca hotels transfer") was excluded from the tours section as it is not a Santa Cruz service.
- Ratings, review counts, and rankings are TripAdvisor data. Before shipping them inside a commercial app, check TripAdvisor's content/licensing terms — names, categories, and your own descriptions are fine, but republishing their ratings at scale may need care.
