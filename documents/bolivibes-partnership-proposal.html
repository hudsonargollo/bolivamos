Strategic Growth Engine: The @parlanaexperiences Partnership Match

Unlocking immediate distribution, cross-promotional volume, and event-driven revenue from launch day.

Since Steff is part of the staff at @parlanaexperiences, we bypass cold outreach. Parlana's language exchanges and cultural events become premier, featured experiences inside BoliVibes, with the partnership agreed before launch and full co-activations rolling out after Fexpocruz.

We use repeatable, cash-flow positive events — especially pool parties, nightlife, and cultural mixers — where venue partners sponsor perks or share ticket/bar revenue. Each activation is designed to generate profit, acquire free users, and finance the next event cycle.

We monetize Parlana's activity pipeline directly via in-app event ticketing (5% commission) and tour bookings (18% marketplace fee): driving tourist sales for Parlana while monetizing BoliVibes traffic.
