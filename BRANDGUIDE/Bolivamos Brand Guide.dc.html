Brand guide & design system

Bolivamos is the event guide for Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia. This guide defines how the brand looks, reads and behaves: logo, color, typography, iconography, UI components and design tokens.

08

Voice & tone

Friendly and local, playful but trustworthy, energetic. Bolivamos informs first; the excitement lives in the design, not in adjectives.

✓ Do

  • "Every public event in Santa Cruz, day by day."
  • "One email every Tuesday. No spam, unsubscribe whenever."
  • Concrete facts: times, prices in Bs, neighborhoods.
  • Short sentences. Active voice.

✕ Don't

  • "The MOST AMAZING event of the year!" — no hype.
  • Corporate or marketing jargon.
  • Emoji in headlines or listings.
  • Promising what isn't verified.
